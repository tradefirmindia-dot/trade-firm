# TRADE FIRM — regular SEO content system

## Live cadence

- Saturday: one original, dated Indian-index market-close note.
- Wednesday: refresh one existing guide using real Search Console questions or a material market/product change.
- Month-end: one deeper research report with a stated data date, method, primary sources, limitations and a stable URL.

## Quality gate before publication

1. Use current NSE, NSE Indices, BSE, SEBI, RBI or issuer filings where the topic requires factual market or regulatory data.
2. State the data date and separate sourced facts from TRADE FIRM interpretation.
3. Add a distinct reader question, not another page targeting the same wording.
4. Link the report to its research pillar, relevant service, methodology and calculator.
5. Check title, description, canonical URL, article dates, schema, sitemap and RSS output.
6. Do not publish a directional promise, invented performance, fake review or unsupported licence claim.
