# TRADE FIRM — genuine authority and backlink playbook

## Objective

Build toward 100 relevant, genuine referring domains over 12 months. This is a campaign target, not a ranking promise. A link counts only when it is live, indexable where appropriate, editorially placed and relevant to TRADE FIRM's real business or published research.

Never buy bulk links, use private blog networks, exchange links at scale, automate forum spam or force exact-match anchors. Google's spam policies can treat manipulative links and low-value scaled pages as violations.

Official guidance:

- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/page-experience

## Campaign 1: verified entity foundation — weeks 1–4

1. Complete genuine business profiles using the same name, website, phone and only confirmed address details.
2. Link official social channels back to `https://www.tradefirm.in` where permitted.
3. Correct duplicates and inconsistent names before creating more listings.
4. Keep a record of login owner, live URL, status and last verification date.

Do not create fake branches, virtual addresses or dozens of low-quality directory profiles.

## Campaign 2: original monthly market reports — months 1–12

Publish one original, dated report every month. Each report needs a clear method, data date, sources, limitations, charts or tables where useful, and a stable canonical URL.

Suggested report series:

- NIFTY and BANK NIFTY monthly market-structure review
- Indian index breadth and sector-leadership snapshot
- Options liquidity and spread observations around expiry
- IPO offer-structure and valuation comparison notes
- Drawdown and position-risk education using transparent examples

Outreach message: explain the one finding relevant to the recipient's audience, link to the exact evidence and offer a chart/table with attribution. Do not send the same generic message to hundreds of sites.

## Campaign 3: expert-source outreach — weekly

1. Monitor requests from legitimate Indian finance, business and local publications.
2. Respond only where TRADE FIRM can add direct expertise.
3. Give a concise answer, one supporting fact or framework and a transparent attribution line.
4. Never invent credentials, regulatory status, performance or client numbers.
5. Record published mentions even when the link is `nofollow`; entity visibility still has business value.

## Campaign 4: resource-page and broken-link outreach — weekly

Search for relevant pages that already recommend risk calculators, Indian market education, IPO checklists or options explainers. Suggest one TRADE FIRM resource only when it materially improves that page.

For broken links, confirm the destination is genuinely unavailable and offer the closest relevant TRADE FIRM page. Do not recreate another publisher's copyrighted article.

## Campaign 5: selective educational contributions — monthly

Pitch one original topic to a publication whose audience overlaps with Indian market education. The contribution should answer a real question and link naturally to a supporting calculator, methodology or detailed guide. Avoid keyword-rich biography links and sites created mainly to sell guest posts.

## Anchor-text guardrails

- 60–70% branded or natural attribution: `TRADE FIRM`, `TRADE FIRM Research Desk`
- 15–20% plain URL or homepage attribution
- 10–15% natural descriptive anchors such as `drawdown recovery calculator`
- Below 5% exact commercial terms, and only when an editor chooses them naturally

These are monitoring guardrails, not a formula to manipulate.

## Prospect qualification

Before outreach, check:

- Is the site topically relevant or genuinely local to the business?
- Does it publish original editorial content?
- Are pages indexed and receiving real search visibility?
- Is the linking page useful without the outbound link?
- Does the site avoid obvious paid-link, casino, adult or unrelated-topic patterns?
- Would a reader benefit from the proposed TRADE FIRM resource?

Third-party authority metrics can help triage but must not be the only decision.

## Weekly tracking sheet

Record:

- Prospect domain and exact target page
- Contact/editor name and source
- Relevance reason
- TRADE FIRM asset proposed
- First contact and follow-up dates
- Status: research, pitched, replied, accepted, live, declined
- Live URL, link attribute and anchor text
- Referral visits and leads
- Notes for relationship follow-up

## 90-day execution target

### Days 1–30

- Verify 10–15 legitimate entity/local profiles.
- Publish the first original report.
- Build a hand-reviewed list of 60 relevant publishers/resources.
- Send 15 personalised pitches.

### Days 31–60

- Publish the second report.
- Send 20 personalised pitches and follow up once where appropriate.
- Offer five expert comments and two educational contributions.
- Review which asset earns replies and strengthen it.

### Days 61–90

- Publish the third report.
- Send 25 personalised pitches to newly qualified prospects.
- Reclaim genuine unlinked brand mentions where the publisher finds a link useful.
- Measure referring domains, referral visits, assisted leads and branded-search growth.

The first objective is not a large raw link count. It is a repeatable system that produces relevant mentions, referral users and authority without creating a future spam-cleanup problem.
