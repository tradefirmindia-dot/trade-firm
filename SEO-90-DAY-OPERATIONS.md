# Trade Firm — 90-day SEO operations system

The website-side foundation is already included in v9.0.0. This file separates what is completed in code from the recurring work that must happen after deployment because Google evaluates the live site over time.

## Completed now

- Six dedicated commercial-intent service pages.
- Twenty-seven complete evergreen guides covering advisory, research, indices, derivatives, equities, IPOs and risk.
- Trust, identity, methodology, author, risk and grievance architecture.
- Organization, service, article, FAQ and breadcrumb structured data.
- Sitemap, robots, RSS, canonical URLs, metadata and deep internal linking.
- Official Thane/Navi Mumbai branch presence, email and phone.

## Days 1–7: deploy and validate

1. Deploy v9.0.0 and confirm every URL in `/sitemap.xml` returns a normal page.
2. In Google Search Console, submit `https://www.tradefirm.in/sitemap.xml` once.
3. Inspect and request indexing for the home page and six priority service pages.
4. Check Page indexing, HTTPS and Core Web Vitals after Google processes the deployment.
5. Add exact branch street addresses only after they are final and consistent across every official listing.

## Days 8–30: establish real entity signals

1. Publish or complete genuine business profiles using the same name, phone, website and confirmed address.
2. Link official social profiles back to tradefirm.in where the platform allows.
3. Seek accurate, editorially earned mentions from relevant finance, local-business and professional sources. Do not buy bulk links.
4. Answer genuine market questions through new, evidence-led articles instead of creating thin city pages.
5. Review Search Console every week for indexing exclusions, query impressions and pages receiving discovery.

## Days 31–60: improve pages from real query data

1. In Search Console Performance, compare queries, pages, countries and devices over 28 days.
2. Add sections only where a real query exposes a missing reader question.
3. Improve titles and descriptions for pages receiving impressions but weak clicks; avoid changing several variables every day.
4. Add internal links from relevant articles to the service page that answers the next reader question.
5. Publish at least one deeply researched, source-supported article each week.

## Days 61–90: build topical authority

1. Refresh pages affected by product, exchange, regulatory or market-structure changes.
2. Create original research formats, explainers or data studies that other publishers have a reason to cite.
3. Strengthen author and editorial transparency with genuine qualifications or licence details only after they can be verified.
4. Compare page-level impressions and average position across 28-day periods, not personal incognito searches.
5. Consolidate overlapping content rather than publishing many pages targeting the same keyword.

## Weekly measurement sheet

Record these fields every week:

- Indexed pages
- Total clicks
- Total impressions
- Average CTR
- Average position
- Top growing queries
- Top growing pages
- New referring domains earned
- Leads from organic landing pages
- Technical or indexing errors

## Non-negotiable quality rules

- Never promise top-10 placement or guaranteed returns.
- Never publish fake reviews, offices, licences, awards or performance.
- Avoid doorway pages that repeat the same content for every Indian city.
- Use official or primary sources for rules, filings and product facts.
- Make risk and limitations visible on financial content.
